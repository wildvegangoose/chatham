Dusenberry House (Presbyterian Parsonage)

📍 186 Main Street, Chatham Borough

The 1848 Greek Revival manse built for forty-five-year pastor Rev. Joseph Meeker Ogden and restored as Chatham's flagship National Register landmark on Main Street.

Mount Vernon School (Red Brick Schoolhouse Museum)

📍 24 Southern Boulevard, Chatham Township

Built on the eve of the Civil War as a rural school downstairs and Sunday chapel upstairs, later Chatham Township Hall, and today the Township's history museum.

Ogden Memorial Presbyterian Church & Library of the Chathams

Horticultural Industry

Chatham's Rose-Growing Greenhouses ("The Rose Capital")

📍 Hillside Avenue, Watchung Avenue, Main Street & Green Village

For nearly a century, acres of steam-heated glass roofs turned Chatham and neighboring Madison into New York's cut-rose garden.

Commuter Rail Landmark

1914 Delaware, Lackawanna & Western Railroad Station

📍 Railroad Plaza & Fairmount Avenue, Chatham Borough

When the Lackawanna eliminated dangerous grade crossings in 1913–14, it lifted the tracks across Chatham on concrete arches and crowned Fairmount Avenue with a stone depot.

1922 school · 1988 Borough Hall
School & Municipal Hall

Chatham Borough Hall (Old Chatham High School)

📍 54 Fairmount Avenue, Chatham Borough

Built in the 1920s commuter boom as Chatham High School, then reborn as Borough Hall when Borough and Township voters united their schools in 1986.

World Boxing Training Camp

Site of Madame Bey's Championship Boxing Camp

📍 516 River Road, Chatham Township

On thirty hilly acres above the Passaic River, a Turkish-Armenian opera singer and former diplomat's wife ran the training camp of heavyweight legends.

Great Swamp National Wildlife Refuge & Wilderness Area

📍 Southern Boulevard, Meyersville & White Bridge Roads, Chatham Township

Remnant of glacial Lake Passaic—saved in 1959–1968 when Chatham and Harding citizens bought 3,000 acres out from under the Port Authority's 10,000-acre super-jetport.
